SECCON Glitch Gate is a hardware challenge where you analyze firmware and develop exploit. Teams can attempt the challenge once you have prepared.

Rule (MUST READ!)
  • You may not remove the polyimide tape (Kapton tape) from the Arduino Nano. This tape is in place to prevent accidental damage to the device.
  • Only attacks using header pins are allowed. Directly attacking the microcontroller's pins is prohibited.
  • Attempting to write to or extract the program from the victim Arduino is prohibited.
  • Each attempt lasts 20 minutes.
  • A team can attempt the challenge once a day. (Up to 2 attempts over 2 days)
  • When your reserved time begins, at lesat 1 and up to 4 team members must go to the designated challenge room.
  • You may leave the room and give up your attempt at any time.
  • You will have access to the challenge hardware and an oscilloscope inside the room.
  • Bringing any additional equipment other than your laptop, phones, and the provided tools is strictly prohibited.
  • You must follow the instructions given by the event staff at all times.
  • If no event staff is present in the challenge room, please notify them immediately.

Check README.md for provided tools.

* If you accidentally break the provided tool or think it is broken, ask event staff for a replacement.

2. Choose desired time to attempt your challenge (JST)
* You cannot cancel your reservation.
* If you have completed your exploit but all slots are full, create a ticket in Discord - we may be able to provide additional slot.
